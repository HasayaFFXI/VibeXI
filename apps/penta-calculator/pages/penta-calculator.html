Distribution of landed-hit damage across all trials · hover a bar for details
Simulates fSTR, dual-stat WSC, flat fTP, the TP-varying accuracy and attack factors, and melee pDIF per hit, matching weaponskills.lua and physical_utilities.lua. Not modelled: the WS stopping early once accumulated damage exceeds the target's HP, shadows / parry / block / guard, PIERCE_SDT and physicalDmgTaken, Sneak Attack's DEX addition, Phalanx and Stoneskin, and skillchains. This estimates raw landed-hit damage. See PentaThrustDamageCalc.md for every input and its source line.
